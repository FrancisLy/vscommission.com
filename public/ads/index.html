VSCommission Ads Analytics

A web application that connects to your own Google Ads account through the official Google Ads API — with read-only access — so you can track spend, account balance, and campaign performance in one simple dashboard.

What is VSCommission Ads Analytics?

VSCommission Ads Analytics helps advertisers monitor their own Google Ads accounts. After you sign in with Google and link an account, we use the official Google Ads API with read-only access to display your data. We never create, edit, or delete campaigns, ads, or budgets, and we never make charges.

How the application uses your Google Ads data

Connect your Google Ads account

Sign in with Google and choose the ad accounts you want to monitor. We read only your account ID and account name — nothing is modified on your behalf.

Track spend and balance

See how much each linked account has spent and what remains of your budgets. Figures come directly from the Google Ads API and refresh in your dashboard.

Monitor campaign metrics

Follow impressions, clicks, click-through rate, and conversions for every campaign, so you always know what is working and where budget goes.

Your data stays yours

Data is used only to show you your own authorized accounts. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties. Revoke access at any time.

How it works

1

Sign in with Google. You review and approve every permission on Google's own consent screen before anything is shared.

2

Link your ad accounts. Pick the Google Ads accounts you want to monitor. Access is strictly read-only.

3

View your dashboard. Spend, balance, and campaign metrics update automatically. Disconnect any time with one click.

Your authorization can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's Third-party apps page, or by emailing info@vscommission.com. See our Privacy Policy for full details, including the Google API Services Limited Use disclosure.
