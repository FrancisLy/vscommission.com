VSCommission is a global affiliate marketing platform connecting brands with publishers. VSCommission Ads Analytics is our companion dashboard that helps advertisers monitor their own Google Ads accounts — account spend, balances, and campaign performance metrics — accessed strictly read-only through the official Google Ads API after explicit user authorization.

  • Connect your Google Ads account with read-only OAuth access
  • Track spend, balances, and campaign metrics across all managed accounts
  • We never create, modify, or delete campaigns, ads, or budgets
